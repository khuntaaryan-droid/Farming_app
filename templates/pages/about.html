About Agri Shop

We are on a mission to empower Indian farmers by connecting them directly with consumers, completely eliminating the middlemen.

Our Vision

To create a transparent, fair, and highly profitable marketplace for farmers where they get the right price for their hard work, and consumers get fresh, high-quality produce directly from the source.

What We Do

  • Provide a platform for farmers to list their produce and machinery.
  • Allow direct communication and negotiation between buyers and sellers.
  • Offer AI-driven insights like the Crop Doctor to help farmers maximize yield.
