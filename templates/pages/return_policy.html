Return & Refund Policy

Fresh Produce

Due to the perishable nature of agricultural produce, we do not accept returns once the delivery is accepted by the buyer.

Quality Disputes

If the produce delivered is completely rotten or severely mismatched from the listing, you must open a dispute within 12 hours of delivery via the Order Details page. Provide photos as evidence.

Refunds

If a dispute is settled in favor of the buyer, refunds for online payments will be processed within 5-7 working days directly to the original payment method.
